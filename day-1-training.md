## Learning Notes
S# Day 1: Cybersecurity Introduction

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications, and data from unauthorized access, damage, or theft.

The **CIA triad** is a basic model used to understand the main goals of information security:

1. **Confidentiality:** Ensures that information is accessible only to authorized people. For example, using access permissions to prevent unauthorized users from viewing private files.
2. **Integrity:** Ensures that information remains accurate and is not changed without authorization. For example, checking a file's hash to detect unexpected changes.
3. **Availability:** Ensures that systems and information are accessible when needed. For example, maintaining backups to help recover from a system failure.

Common cybersecurity threats include phishing, malware, weak passwords, unauthorized access, and denial-of-service attacks.

Cybersecurity ethics means acting responsibly, respecting privacy, following applicable laws, and testing only systems that you own or have explicit permission to assess.
