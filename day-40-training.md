### 4. Cryptography and AES Project

One of the main parts of my training was learning about cryptography and an AES-GCM file-encryption project.

I studied:
- Symmetric and asymmetric encryption.
- AES encryption.
- Encryption keys and nonce management.
- AES-GCM authentication.
- File encryption and decryption.
- Wrong-key and modified-ciphertext testing.
- File integrity and safe error handling.
- Key protection and secure storage.

I learned that AES-GCM provides both confidentiality and authentication. I also learned that a nonce must be unique for each encryption under the same key.
